Just Share Stuff - Mobile Application Project SDG 12(Responsible production and consumption)

Problem Statement:
University communities often waste usable items such as books, electronics, clothing and more because there is no simple platform for students and staff to resell or donate them. This increases unnecessary waste and environmental impact. 
My solution is a campus marketplace app that encourages reselling and donating used items to promote sustainable consumption and reduce wastage.

Solution :
The application provides a dedicated campus marketplace platform where students and staff can resell or donate usable items instead of throwing them away. By making second-hand items easier to discover and trade within the university community, the app helps reduce unnecessary waste, promotes sustainable consumption and encourages a reuse culture aligned with SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.

Features:
1. User profile management
2. Browse and explore second-hand or donation items
3. Product search and category filtering
4. Product details page with item information
5. Offer price to buy and cart system for item transactions
6. Donation request feature for free items
7. Seller and buyer meetup arrangement
8. Transaction status tracking (awaiting seller, accepted offer, rejected offer, completed)
